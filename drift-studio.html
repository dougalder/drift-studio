Motion brush

Longer, steady strokes give smoother flow. Hold Option (Alt) while painting to erase. Speed applies to the whole selected layer.

Freeze brush keep things perfectly still

Frozen areas show the original pixels, so a person or object never wobbles. Use a low feather for crisp edges.

Mask brush hide parts of the layer

Move

Drag the picture to move it. Drag a corner square to resize, or the round handle to tilt; hold Shift to snap to 15°. Pinch or scroll also resizes.

    How Drift works

    Everything stays on your device. Your photos are opened and processed by your own browser. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and no one else can see your work.

    Nothing is saved between visits. Closing or reloading the page clears your layers and painting, so export the video before you leave.

    1. New or Paste an image (you can also drop one on the picture).
    2. Paint with Motion in the direction things should flow, and Freeze anything that must stay still.
    3. Add more images in Layers, each with its own speed, mask and blend mode.
    4. Export a seamless MP4 or animated GIF loop and save it.
